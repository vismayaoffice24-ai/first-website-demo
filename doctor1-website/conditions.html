Kidney Conditions Treated

Early identification of renal problems allows for interventions that can protect nephrons and maintain your overall vitality. Select any condition pill or card below to learn about warning signs, diagnostic tests, causes, and treatment approaches with Dr. Neelima in Kannur.

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) →

A gradual, progressive loss of filtering ability that often produces no obvious symptoms in its early stages. Diagnosed via blood tests (serum creatinine, eGFR) and urine tests (protein/albumin-to-creatinine ratio).

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

Acute Kidney Injury (AKI) →

A sudden drop in renal function occurring over hours or days, frequently triggered by severe dehydration, severe infections, medication side effects, or urinary obstruction. Often reversible with prompt medical intervention.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

Kidney Stones (Nephrolithiasis) →

Hard crystalline mineral deposits that crystallize within the renal pelvis or calyces. Characterized by severe flank or lower abdominal pain, blood in urine, and burning sensations. Prevention requires metabolic evaluation.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

Diabetic Kidney Disease (Nephropathy) →

Kidney damage resulting from long-standing elevated blood sugar, causing thickening of the glomerular basement membrane and protein leakage into urine. Annual screening is essential for all diabetic patients.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

High Blood Pressure & Kidney Health →

Hypertension can cause kidney damage, and preexisting kidney disease can significantly worsen blood pressure. Careful renoprotective blood pressure management preserves renal vasculature.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

Glomerular Diseases →

Disorders affecting the microscopic filters (glomeruli) within the kidney, resulting in protein leakage (nephrotic syndrome) or red blood cells in the urine (nephritic syndrome), accompanied by facial or leg swelling.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

Kidney Transplant Care →

Comprehensive pre-transplant medical assessment, compatibility preparation, and vigilant long-term post-transplant follow-up to optimize allograft survival, regulate immunosuppressant levels, and minimize infection risks.

Read signs, causes & FAQ →

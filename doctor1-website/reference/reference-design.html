Reviews & FAQ

Patient stories

Patient stories will be shared here with their consent.

When should I see a kidney specialist?

If your tests show a change in kidney function, you have swelling, changes in urine, or ongoing high blood pressure or diabetes, a nephrologist can help.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Bring your previous reports, a list of your medicines and any questions you have.

Does a high creatinine always mean kidney disease?

Not always. Creatinine can change for several reasons, so the result is read together with your history and other tests.

How can I protect my kidneys every day?

Keep blood pressure and blood sugar in check, stay hydrated, avoid unnecessary painkillers and get regular check-ups.

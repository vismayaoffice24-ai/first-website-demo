What to bring to your first consultation

To help Dr. Neelima thoroughly evaluate your kidney health, please bring previous blood and urine test reports (especially serum creatinine, eGFR, and urine routine/protein tests), recent ultrasound or CT scans, a complete list of your current prescription medications, and any questions you have written down.

Patient-Centered Care at MIMS Hospital, Kannur

Navigating kidney conditions can feel overwhelming. Dr. Neelima focuses on continuous, empathetic support at every stage.

Time to Listen

Consultations are unhurried, giving you ample time to describe symptoms, discuss lifestyle concerns, and have each test explained thoroughly.

Coordinated Care

Seamless collaboration with cardiologists, endocrinologists, and dietitians at MIMS Hospital to manage diabetes, hypertension, and kidney health together.

Transparent Guidance

No vague jargon or unsupported claims. You receive clear, honest medical assessments tailored to your unique kidney function stage.

Ongoing Support

From early preventive screening to long-term dialysis supervision and post-transplant follow-up, your renal journey is guided consistently.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

Dr. Neelima ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy and confidentiality of patients visiting this website and consulting at MIMS Hospital, Kannur. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal and health-related details.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website to schedule a consultation or make an enquiry, you may provide contact and clinical information, including:

  • Full name
  • Telephone and mobile numbers
  • Email address (optional)
  • Preferred consultation dates and time windows
  • Type of visit requested (in-person, follow-up, report review)
  • Brief summary of symptoms or clinical reasons for visit

2. How Your Information Is Used

The details you submit through our appointment or contact forms are used strictly and solely for:

  • Coordinating and scheduling your OPD appointment at MIMS Hospital, Kannur.
  • Direct doctor-patient communication regarding your clinical inquiry or visit preparation.
  • Answering questions regarding clinic timings, reports to bring, or directions.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade patient personal information to any third parties or marketing brokers under any circumstances.

3. WhatsApp Communication & Consent

Our website utilizes WhatsApp messaging (wa.me) as an accessible, direct channel for appointment scheduling and clinical communication. When you click "Book via WhatsApp" or "Send Query on WhatsApp", your entered details are pre-formatted into a message that opens within your own WhatsApp client. Sending this message constitutes your voluntary consent to share these details directly with our clinic scheduling desk.

4. Patient Testimonials & Consent Policy

In accordance with the Kerala State Medical Council (KSMC) and Indian medical ethical guidelines, patient privacy is strictly protected. Patient stories, clinical case summaries, or testimonials will only ever be shared on this website after obtaining explicit, informed, and written patient consent.

5. Data Security

We implement standard technical measures to safeguard the integrity of our website. Please be aware that while we strive to protect your data, transmission across cellular and internet networks is subject to the security measures of your chosen communication provider (such as WhatsApp/Meta).

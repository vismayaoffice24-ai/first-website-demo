Clinical Kidney Care Services

Dr. Neelima provides evidence-based, compassionate nephrology care for patients across Kannur and northern Kerala. Click any service card below to read complete clinical details, who it helps, what to expect, and related FAQs.

Kidney Function Evaluation

A comprehensive assessment of your kidneys' filtering ability using serum creatinine, eGFR, urine protein tests, and renal imaging, explained in clear, understandable terms.

Dialysis Care

Kidney Disease Management

Structured, individualized medical management designed to preserve remaining kidney function, optimize blood pressure and blood sugar, and slow disease progression.

Kidney Transplant Follow-up

Blood Pressure Management

Kidney-conscious blood pressure control focusing on renoprotection, secondary hypertension diagnostics, medication reviews, and dietary sodium guidance.

Preventive Kidney Care

Early screening, lifestyle guidance, and regular kidney check-ups for individuals with diabetes, hypertension, family history of renal disease, or prolonged medication use.

Need guidance on which service you require?

If you have recent blood or urine test reports showing elevated creatinine, protein in the urine, or unexplained swelling, Dr. Neelima can review your medical records and outline a step-by-step diagnostic and care plan.
